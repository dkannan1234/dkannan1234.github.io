Published Senior Thesis: Impact of Rideshare Platforms on Social Hierarchies in Bengaluru

Published research · Penn Urban Studies

Investigating how rideshare platforms shape social hierarchies, transportation inequality, and driver labor in Bengaluru. Published in the Spring 2026 Barnard-Columbia Urban Review, page 53.

Read the story: Published Senior Thesis: Impact of Rideshare Platforms on Social Hierarchies in Bengaluru

Selected through a highly competitive research grant process, I received a $3,500 Hassenfeld Grant to support my senior thesis.
